# Rift Runner
A one-arena, top-down hovercraft sandbox. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

- **Touch:** hold one of the eight direction buttons to steer. Tap FIRE for one shot. Auto Aim tracks traffic cones but never shoots for you. Turn it off to drag the separate aim pad and release for one shot. The controls stay below the arena.
- **Steering:** the ship keeps its momentum when you release the controls. Steer against the drift to brake or reverse.
- **Desktop:** WASD flies; arrows or mouse aim; press Space or click once per shot. Escape/P pauses.
- **Sound and shake:** use the switches at the top. Audio starts when you launch a run. Use Test blaster to try the cannon sound before launch. Reduced-motion settings default shake off.

The hovercraft and cones physically collide. Drive into cones to push them, or hit them with the blaster to send them sliding and spinning. The apron is a sandbox: no opponents, extra stages, or upgrades. Local settings carry over.

## Cannon audio
The in-game cannon uses a short mix of [Game Blaster Shot by Universfield](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-game-blaster-shot-635112/) under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), trimmed, retimed, filtered and layered with an original bass recoil. The project bundles the gameplay mix, not the original download.

## iPhone Home Screen
Open the deployed HTTPS game in Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen, and enable Open as Web App if shown. This is optional; the normal browser version still works. The installed shortcut needs an internet connection and the same site access as Safari. No private page or sign-in data is cached for offline play.

## Thruster audio
Use **Test engine** to hear the synthesized attack, sustain and fade independently of **Test blaster**. Movement input drives the engine; coasting does not. Turn Sound on and start the game or tap a test button to unlock browser audio.

If you leave the game or lock your iPhone, audio restarts on your next tap after returning. Use **Resume flight** to continue, or either test button to check sound. Your Sound setting does not change.
