# Crosswind class
The Crosswind class is Rift Runner’s standard flight-deck frigate: a twin hull with a center runway and four exterior fighter pads. Shared art, footprint and pad coordinates are in `frigate-hulls.js`.

| Variant | Ship identity | Affiliation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free People | **Crosswind**, Captain Nessa Rourke | Independent of Pegasus and Bifrost | Live chart contact; removed from Pegasus Free Flight |
| Pirate | **Red Grin**, its own weathered crimson paint | Pirates | Pirate design preview; not spawned |

Do not change Crosswind’s name, captain, planet route or access rules when adding a pirate variant. Shared dimensions and pads are not a grant of pirate landing permission. Choose pirate equipment and behavior separately. Pegasus and Resolute use separate flight corridors. Crosswind dock geometry is reserved for a dedicated independent encounter. A future spawned Red Grin needs its own corridor and approach policies.
