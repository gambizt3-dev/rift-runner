# Pirate convoy playtest

**Entry:** Pegasus Hangar → Simulator: Convoy Escort, or select that flight mode from the cockpit. The simulator supplies a fighter and never writes credits, kills, hull damage, carrier stores or Galaxy Map state. The separate Red Grin duel retains its frigate, shield and broadside.

The Civilian Freighter takes about 50 seconds to reach the northern extraction gate if it never has to stop. It holds when the escort leaves the area. The threat is one unshielded Blackwake Raider, a repurposed pirate vessel with 30 hull and one turret. It fires one telegraphed shot, then reloads. It does not ram or fire broadsides. Players can intercept the shot, draw the turret's attention or disable the pirate. The freighter must reach the gate even if the pirate is destroyed. Losing a ship or missing extraction ends the run and the debrief offers a rematch.

Check on a phone that the green route, turret warning and hold status are legible. A future cooperative version needs one shared convoy and outcome, not two synchronized solo simulators. The alien blockade is a separate playtest and this simulator does not change any live Galaxy Map events.
