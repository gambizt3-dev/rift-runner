# Pirate convoy playtest

**Entry:** Pegasus Hangar → Simulator: Convoy Escort, or select that flight mode from the cockpit. The simulator supplies a fighter and never writes credits, kills, hull damage, carrier stores, or Galaxy Map state. The existing Red Grin duel is unchanged.

Follow the green route north with the Civilian Freighter. It holds when its escort leaves the area. Red Grin warns before a broadside; a pilot can draw the next volley by getting between the ships and moving off the freighter's line, intercept the incoming bolts, or disable the frigate. The freighter must reach the extraction gate even if Red Grin is destroyed. Losing either the freighter or the escort ends the run. The debrief reports cargo hull and distance and offers a rematch.

Compare a pilot chasing the frigate with one watching the convoy. The playtest passes if the escort changes how the player flies and the loss screen makes the mistake clear. Test phone text readability, aiming, progress, and rematch before making this a shared Galaxy Map event. A future cooperative version needs one shared convoy and outcome, not two synchronized solo simulators.

Keep the alien objective distinct: a single alien craft locks down a planet, and clearing the blockade restores the affected services on the Galaxy Map. Do not turn that into another cargo escort. This playtest does not alter alien events.
