# Shared fleet flight beta

The beta is an opt-in entry on the public Galaxy Map. Select the carrier where your fighter is parked and choose **Shared fleet flight · Beta**. Use a repaired, fitted, owned fighter and a server-confirmed berth. To confirm a first berth, dock and **Store Inside** at your carrier in ordinary Free Flight.

On the beta page, choose **Go to launch deck → Launch**. A second pilot stationed on the same carrier can do the same and see your ship in the server-backed exterior. Use arrows or the touch buttons to fly. Approach the orange pad, release thrust in its inner ring, then **Request Docking → Store Inside**. Storing removes the ship from the shared exterior and sends the pilot to the actual private Hangar on that carrier. Pegasus and Bifrost have separate rooms and recovery pads.

The beta uses a simplified flight view and an isolated fleet state table. It does not change the fighter's career health, fit, rewards, balance, or confirmed berth. It does not replace ordinary Free Flight, solo or mission modes. No active combat, visiting another fleet from this page, or persistent unattended NPC traffic is included.

Use two independent browser profiles to test. Accounts are browser-local; two tabs in the same ordinary profile are not two independent pilots. See the [living fleet guide](LIVING-BATTLE-FLEET-MAP.md) for rollout stages.
