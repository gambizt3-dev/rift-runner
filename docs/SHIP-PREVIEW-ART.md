# Top-down catalogue art

The ship pictures are selectable gallery previews. They do not grant ownership, create NPCs, or enable rentals, flight or interior views. The signed-off circular marketplace is a fixed station concept outside this catalogue.

Visual direction: longer, slimmer hulls with small command bridges instead of fighter canopies. Use contrasting builders' styles: smooth civilian shells, exposed equipment on industrial workhorses, and obvious patches on repurposed pirate ships. Cruisers can show a stepped command deck or a smoother lower profile and centre spine. Class cards normalize framing for artwork inspection; they are not a scale chart and do not establish gameplay dimensions.

References were used for broad design language, not copied assets: Damen Fast Crew Supplier, Naval Group Gowind proportions, and the smooth versus exposed structure contrast in Star Citizen. All gallery assets are original concepts with no Star Citizen ships or imagery in the game files.
