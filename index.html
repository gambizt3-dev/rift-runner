Settings
Optional touch aim assist. Using the right aim pad takes over until you reset the field or re-enable Auto Aim.

Asteroid cover adds a new field of movable rocks and scrap each duel. Both sides’ shots can be blocked.

Allows game sound in Silent Mode where supported and may pause other media. The Sound switch still controls the game.

Hold the center thruster to push where the fighter points. The outer arrows trim movement, with more effect when you slow down. Drag the right pad to aim. Tap the pause icon to pause flight.

Keyboard E main thruster · WASD to trim · arrows to aim · Space or click to fire · hold Space for A-Alkine · 1 or 2 to select primary or secondary gun · Q for the fitted shield · P or Esc to pause

Tap to turn formation off or on. In Wingman vs Vandul, Formation holds your slot while the lead scouts north. Holding main thrust switches formation off.
Tap to fire. In normal A-Alkine mode, hold to repeat. In Burst mode, tap for six shots with Sterling or A-Alkine, or eighteen with AK77. Wait for cooldown. Sterling costs 1 He3 per shot.
Hold the center thruster to accelerate in the direction the fighter points. Release to keep gliding. Use the outer arrows or ring to trim movement. Powered maneuvering uses He3.
Touch or drag to aim independently. Releasing keeps your heading and does not fire.
